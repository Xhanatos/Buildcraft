# Buildcraft

A standalone Unity Mod Manager mod for **Pathfinder: Wrath of the Righteous** that adds new classes and archetypes and reworks existing class progressions.

## Requirements

- Pathfinder: Wrath of the Righteous 2.7.0x
- Unity Mod Manager 0.27.11 or newer

BlueprintCore is merged into the compiled mod. TabletopTweaks and other gameplay mods are not required.

## Unity Mod Manager settings

Open Buildcraft in Unity Mod Manager to enable or disable its content in
six groups: global changes, added backgrounds, races, class and archetype
changes, added feats and exploits, and spells or spell-list changes. Every
changed class family, every added background, every added feat, every added
spell, the playable Goblin race, the Half-Orc ability-score change, and every race's alternate heritage
module has an individual
switch. All options are enabled by default.

Settings are applied while Wrath builds its blueprint cache. Use **Save
settings**, close the game completely, and restart it after changing an option.
Respec affected characters after changing a progression option. Disabling
custom content already selected by a character is not recommended until that
character has been respecced without it.

## Design decisions

- Dread Archer is a Fighter archetype built around ranged fear pressure. It
  exchanges Bravery for scaling demoralize bonuses, selected bonus feats for
  Deadly Aim, Painful Shots, Merciless, and Dreadful Carnage, and standard
  Weapon Training for an all-ranged-weapon progression. It is proficient with
  light and medium armor but not heavy armor or shields.

- Tabletop-compatible archetype stacking is enabled by default under Global
  Changes. When first taking a class during character creation or a respec,
  compatible archetypes of that same class may be selected together. Wrath's
  native `CanAddArchetype` check remains authoritative, so archetypes that
  replace the same class feature cannot stack. Existing classes cannot gain a
  later archetype without a respec. Class names, descriptions, skills,
  statistics, spellbooks, and progression previews include the full selected
  stack. If ToyBox's equivalent option is enabled or the standalone Multiple
  Archetypes mod is loaded, Buildcraft automatically suspends its
  overlapping runtime patches to avoid double-patching.
- When ToyBox's experimental Multiple Classes On Level-Up option is active,
  Buildcraft keeps its class-list radio selection synchronized with the
  primary class in Wrath's level-up controller. Transient previews without a
  valid primary class are excluded from ToyBox's BAB, save, and hit-point
  aggregation until the primary class has been restored.
- Every new or fully respecced character may choose up to two different Traits at
  the first character level from a pool of seventy-five implemented options. The
  pool is organized into Combat, Faith, Magic, Social, and Racial categories, and
  each trait slot has independent category selectors so both traits may come from
  the same category. Each selection also contains an independent None option,
  allowing either or both choices to be skipped without changing the Unity Mod
  Manager settings. Forty-four
  of those options are racial traits and appear only for their associated
  Human, Elf, Dwarf, Gnome, Halfling, Half-Elf, Half-Orc, Aasimar, Tiefling,
  Oread, Dhampir, or Kitsune race. The two obligatory Trait choices are added to the
  character-creation roadmap once a race has been selected; they are feature
  selection phases rather than a permanently visible top-row category.
  Adopted opens one further obligatory choice containing the forty-four racial
  traits belonging to races other than the character's own. This
  system has a Global Changes toggle and never grants additional picks when
  multiclassing. The one-time general feat Additional Traits grants two further
  picks from the same pool and has its own Added Feats toggle. Tabletop social
  and technical skills are mapped to Wrath's Persuasion and Trickery skills;
  traits that make one of two skills a class skill open a second choice for it.
- Fate's Favored increases each positive luck bonus affecting its owner by 1.
  Pragmatic Activator and Bruising Intellect replace the relevant Charisma
  modifier with Intelligence for Use Magic Device and demoralizing checks.
  Two-World Magic adds one foreign cantrip to a selected spellcasting class,
  while Gifted Adept raises caster level by 1 for one selected spell.
- Kitsune can select Clever Predator, Two Faces One Mind, Hidden Tail, and
  Vulpine Ambusher as racial Traits. Keen Kitsune remains Wrath's native ability
  heritage; Skilled Kitsune and Fast Shifter are alternate racial heritages and
  therefore exchange native Kitsune abilities instead of using Trait slots.
- Thirty-four race options are implemented as alternate racial heritages, not
  campaign Traits. Core races extend their native heritage selection; Human,
  Kitsune, Aasimar, Tiefling, Oread, Dhampir, and Goblin receive one separate obligatory
  heritage exchange during character creation. Standard Heritage retains the
  normal package, while every alternative tooltip states the features it loses.
  Keen Kitsune, Pyromaniac, and Dual-Minded reuse Wrath's native options.
- Goblin is a selectable Small race using Owlcat's native Goblin body, head,
  animations, equipment support, and three body presets. Both gender choices
  use the Goblin mesh's matching skeleton. Its Looks page also supports skin,
  eye, scar, warpaint, tattoo, and outfit-color customization.
  Its racial modifiers
  are +4 Dexterity, -2 Strength, and -2 Charisma; its standard abilities grant
  +4 Stealth, +2 Perception, and 30-foot movement. Cave Crawler, City
  Scavenger, Eat Anything, Hard Head Big Teeth, Over-Sized Ears, and Tree
  Runner are available as alternate racial heritages.
- The Worldwound background category contains Sarkorian Exile, Wardstone
  Veteran, Redeemed Cultist, Kenabres Watchman, Numerian Salvager, Temple
  Weaponmaster, Crusade Quartermaster, and Worldwound Cartographer. Each has an
  individual Unity Mod Manager toggle. Their nested choices are registered only
  in the background tree and never in race-heritage selections.
- The general feat list now includes Extra Hex (Witch), Extra Hex (Shaman),
  Extra Revelation, Horse Master, Hurtful, Dirty Fighting, Guided Hand,
  Erastil's Blessing, Split Hex, Shield Brace, Two-Weapon Defense, Armor of the
  Pit, Greater Unarmed Strike, the Eldritch Heritage chain, Feral Combat
  Training, Racial Heritage, Artful Dodge, Cut from the Air, and Hex Strike.
  Cursing Gaze, Ricochet, Bashing Bulwark, and Shielded Casting are available
  from the Mythic Ability selection. Combat feats
  in this package are also available from the native Fighter combat-feat pool.
- Guided Hand derives deity favored weapons from Owlcat's Warpriest deity map,
  requires Channel Energy and a deity, and intentionally has no Channel Smite
  prerequisite. Extra Hex and Extra Revelation reuse the native class
  selections so their option prerequisites and later game updates are retained.
- Split Hex uses a default-on toggle and a manually aimed free second use in the
  same round. It excludes Major and Grand Hexes and rejects the first target.
- Raking Claws requires base attack bonus +4 and has no Wild Shape or Major Form prerequisite. Its bleed effect remains unchanged.
- Shield Brace supports the native spear and polearm fighter groups with light,
  heavy, and tower shields. The shield's armor check penalty applies to the
  braced weapon's attacks.
- Mighty Hurling uses Strength instead of Dexterity for attack rolls with
  throwing axes, darts, javelins, and shuriken when Strength is higher.
  Crushing Throw extends Power Attack to those thrown weapons with one-handed
  scaling and half damage on off-hand throws.
- Balanced Grip is a repeatable weapon-specific combat feat for one-handed
  slashing or piercing weapons. It enables Weapon Finesse and Mythic Weapon
  Finesse and treats the selected weapon as light for two-weapon fighting
  penalties without changing its proficiency, damage dice, or weapon group.
- Two-Weapon Defense grants +1 dodge AC while wielding two weapons or a double
  weapon and +2 while Fighting Defensively. Armor of the Pit gives Tieflings
  +2 natural armor AC. Greater Unarmed Strike establishes 1d8/1d10 minimum
  unarmed damage and advances Monk unarmed damage by four effective levels.
- Long Arm is added as a 1st-level spell or extract to the Alchemist,
  Bard/Skald, Bloodrager, Magus, Witch,
  Wizard/Sorcerer/Arcanist/Eldritch Scoundrel, and Magic Deceiver spell lists,
  as well as the Nature Mage's arcane spellbook. It is a personal
  Transmutation lasting 1 minute per caster level and increases melee reach by
  5 feet. Nature Mage receives an isolated copy of its Druid-derived list so
  ordinary Druids do not gain the spell.
- Blistering Invective is a 2nd-level Alchemist, Bard/Skald, and Inquisitor
  spell. Arcane Concordance is a 3rd-level Bard/Skald spell with Extend and
  Reach variants. Burst of Radiance is a 2nd-level Cleric, Druid, and
  Wizard/Sorcerer/Arcanist spell. Blade Tutor's Spirit is a 1st-level Magus
  spell and a 2nd-level Paladin and Wizard/Sorcerer/Arcanist spell. Deadly
  Juggernaut is a 3rd-level Cleric, Inquisitor, and Paladin spell; qualifying
  melee kills build up to +5 luck on melee attacks, melee weapon damage,
  Strength checks, and Athletics, plus DR 10/-.
- Shillelagh is a 1st-level Druid, Hunter, Ranger, and Shaman Transmutation.
  For 1 minute per caster level it gives magical and nonmagical clubs and
  quarterstaffs wielded by the caster +1 enhancement and damage as though two
  size categories larger, including both ends of a quarterstaff.
- Weapon of Awe is a 2nd-level Cleric/Oracle, Inquisitor, Paladin, and
  Warpriest spell. Sanctify Armor is a 3rd-level Paladin and 4th-level
  Inquisitor spell. Forceful Strike is a 4th-level Cleric/Oracle, Inquisitor,
  Paladin, and Warpriest spell. Wrathful Weapon is a 4th-level Cleric/Oracle
  and Warpriest spell with anarchic, axiomatic, holy, and unholy variants.
- Alchemists can select Bone-Spike Mutagen from level 6. While any tier of
  Mutagen is active it grants +2 natural armor and one additional masterwork
  natural spike attack dealing 1d6 piercing damage. Mutation Warriors can
  select it from level 7.
- Collective Memory requires Cognatogen and adds half the character's Alchemist
  level to all four Knowledge and Lore skills while any tier of Cognatogen is
  active. Pheromones grants a permanent +4 competence bonus on Persuasion.
- Every Alchemist gains Awakened Intellect at level 20 as a permanent +4
  Intelligence increase. True Cognatogen replaces it in the Grand Discovery
  list and grants +8 natural armor and +8 Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma
  while applying the Cognatogen penalties to physical ability scores.
- These discoveries are included in the standard, Extra Discovery,
  Vivisectionist, and Extra Vivisectionist lists. Bone-Spike Mutagen and
  Collective Memory also appear for Mutation Warriors; Underground Chemist's
  curated discovery list is unchanged.
- Fighters gain three free Weapon Focus selections at levels 1, 4, and 7, followed by three free Greater Weapon Focus selections at levels 8, 11, and 14. Normal feat prerequisites remain enforced, and Fighter archetypes inherit all six grants.
- Aldori Defender replaces the level-1 Weapon Focus selection with an automatic Weapon Focus (Dueling Sword) grant. Its later Weapon Focus and Greater Weapon Focus selections remain unrestricted.
- Each rank of Fighter Armor Training reduces armor check penalty by 2 and increases the armor's maximum Dexterity allowance by 2, twice the original per-rank values. Existing movement-speed benefits are unchanged.
- Tower Shield Specialists gain I Am Your Shield at level 1. While wielding a tower shield, they can activate this swift-action toggle for 2 rounds per day plus their positive Constitution modifier, gaining 2 more rounds at Fighter levels 5/10/15/20. Adjacent allies receive the specialist's current tower shield AC bonus, including enhancement, as a Shield bonus; the daily rounds need not be consecutive.
- Tower Shield Specialist's level-9 Tower Shield Defense includes a magic tower shield's enhancement bonus, as well as its base shield bonus and Shield Focus, when adding shield AC against touch attacks.
- Each rank of the Two-Handed Fighter's existing Strong Grip feature also grants a +1 inherent Strength bonus at levels 2, 6, 10, 14, and 18, reaching +5. The separate Strong Arms feature has been removed.
- Dragonheir Scion's Fearful Might grants +2 on Intimidate checks at level 2 and increases to +4/+6/+8/+10 at Fighter levels 6/10/14/18. Dragonheir Scions also retain the base Fighter's Armor Mastery at level 19 and Weapon Mastery at level 20 alongside their archetype abilities.
- "Turns" in the original design are implemented as rounds.
- Focused Aim is a swift-action supernatural ability.
- Crimson Marksman retains normal Fighter Bonus Feats except for the level-6 selection, and retains Bravery and Weapon Mastery. It loses Armor Training and Armor Mastery.
- Crimson Marksman is proficient with all simple and martial weapons and light armor, but not medium armor, heavy armor, or shields unless those proficiencies come from another source.
- Standard selectable weapon training at levels 5, 9, 13, and 17 is replaced by Ranged Weapon Training, which applies to all ranged weapons.
- Crimson Marksman's Ranged Weapon Training is granted only by its archetype progression and is excluded from global Weapon Training selections such as Disciple of the Pike's spear-or-polearm choice.
- Blood in the Eyes refreshes rather than stacks.
- Feast on Their Screams stacks, with each five-round stack expiring independently.
- All Barbarians except Invulnerable Ragers follow the Damage Reduction pacing at levels 1, 4, 7, 10, 13, 16, and 19, reaching DR 7/-. Mad Dogs use their companion-sharing version at those same levels; all others receive the standard feature.
- Existing placements and archetype removals of standard Barbarian Damage Reduction are normalized so the progression cannot grant missing or duplicate ranks; Invulnerable Rager and Mad Dog use the targeted replacements described below.
- At level 8, all Barbarians gain Battleborn. Each enemy they personally kill restores one round of their rage resource, even while rage is inactive. Instinctual Warrior's Focused Rage resource is supported.
- Every native version of Danger Sense—Barbarian, Rogue, and Archaeologist—retains its existing trap bonuses and also applies its current rank as a dodge bonus to AC and a Reflex-save bonus against effects originating from enemies that are invisible to the character.
- Armored Hulk retains the base Barbarian's normal Uncanny Dodge feature at level 2, but still loses Improved Uncanny Dodge.
- Flesheaters retain both the base Barbarian's Uncanny Dodge at level 2 and Improved Uncanny Dodge at level 5.
- At level 1, Beastkin Berserkers gain Feral Swiftness with the Fast Movement icon. While any tier of Feral Transformation is active, it grants a +10-foot untyped movement-speed bonus that stacks with other bonuses.
- Invulnerable Rager's personal Damage Reduction gains ten ranks at levels 1, 3, 5, 7, 9, 11, 13, 15, 17, and 19, reaching DR 10/- and replacing the standard Barbarian DR progression.
- At level 10, Invulnerable Ragers gain Just a Scratch. While below 50% health, it grants 2 additional stackable DR/-.
- Extreme Endurance grants electricity resistance in addition to its existing fire and cold resistance, using the same 1-to-6 scaling.
- Mad Dogs and their animal companions gain the Mad Dog damage reduction ranks at the standard Barbarian levels 1, 4, 7, 10, 13, 16, and 19. Pack Ragers inherit the same standard pacing directly.
- Bards gain Bard Talents at levels 2, 5, 8, 11, 14, 17, and 20. Combat Trick remains repeatable from the Bard Talent list.
- Jack of All Trades is gained at Bard level 1 and grants one additional skill point for each Bard level in addition to its existing +1 bonus on all skill checks. Archetypes that trade the feature away retain their tradeoff and normal skill-point rate.
- Dance of a Hundred Cuts is a 5th-level Bard/Skald Transmutation spell lasting one round per Bard or Skald level. It grants +3 melee attack and +3 dodge AC only while a Bardic Performance or native Skald Raging Song is actively running; Lingering Performance alone does not qualify.
- Bards can learn True Strike and Magic Weapon as 1st-level spells and Greater Magic Weapon as a 3rd-level spell.
- Archaeologist's Luck has twice its normal resource progression: 8 + twice the Archaeologist's Charisma modifier at level 1, plus 4 rounds for every later Bard level.
- At level 20, Archaeologists gain True Luck. Once per round, their first failed attack roll, saving throw, skill check, or spell resistance check is rerolled with a luck bonus equal to half their Charisma modifier, taking the better result.
- Bards that retain Deadly Performance gain True Artist at level 20: +2 on all
  skill checks, +2 to Bardic Performance saving throw DCs, and +4 Dexterity
  while a Bardic Performance is active.
- Archaeologist Danger Sense gains the same rank-scaled AC and Reflex-save benefits against invisible enemies as Barbarian and Rogue Danger Sense.
- Flame Dancer's Fire Dance also grants allies under the performance 1d4 fire damage on weapon, natural, and unarmed attacks at level 3, increasing to 1d8 at level 6 and 1d10 at level 11. Its original fire resistance remains unchanged.
- Bloodragers gain Consuming Rage at level 8. As a swift action, they can sacrifice an available level 1-4 Bloodrager spell slot to restore the same number of Bloodrage rounds, up to their normal maximum; Bloodrage need not be active.
- Steelblood Bloodragers retain the standard Bloodrager Damage Reduction ranks at levels 7, 10, 13, 16, and 19, reaching DR 5/-. Their other archetype feature exchanges remain unchanged.
- Spell Eater Bloodragers retain normal Uncanny Dodge at level 2 but continue to lose Improved Uncanny Dodge at level 5.
- Hag-Riven Bloodragers retain normal Uncanny Dodge at level 2 but continue to lose Improved Uncanny Dodge at level 5.
- At level 17, Hag-Riven's Claws of the Hag has a 17-20 critical threat range. This applies only to the archetype's own Medium and Small claw weapons.
- Cavaliers gain Cavalier Bonus Feats at levels 2, 6, 10, 14, and 18 instead of 6, 12, and 18. Cavalier archetypes inherit this schedule, while Gendarme continues to replace every normal Cavalier Bonus Feat with its own feat selection.
- Order of the Cockatrice's Braggart grants its +2 attack bonus against targets under fear effects as a profane bonus instead of a morale bonus.
- Order of the Shroud's scaling attack bonus against an undead challenge target is a sacred bonus instead of a morale bonus.
- Order of the Star's scaling bonuses to all three saving throws while threatening its challenge target are sacred bonuses instead of morale bonuses.
- All fifteen Order of the Sword By My Honor choices grant their +2 selected saving-throw bonus as a luck bonus instead of a morale bonus.
- Fearsome Leader's Fearmonger grants +2 on Intimidate checks at level 3 and another +2 every three Cavalier levels thereafter.
- Gendarme gains Glorious Charge at level 9. Completing a charge grants a two-round morale bonus to all saving throws equal to the Gendarme's Charisma modifier; the charge need not hit, and repeated charges refresh rather than stack the buff.
- Ghost Rider's Frightful Gaze is a swift action at every level, including after its level-9 upgrade against fear-immune and mind-affecting-immune creatures.
- Knight of the Wall's Deflective Shield and Soul Shield include a magic shield's enhancement bonus when calculating the shield bonus added to touch AC and CMD or Will saves, respectively.
- Standard Bearer's Banner of Solace is a swift action instead of a full-round action. Awesome Pennon's +1 attack bonus and +3 saving-throw bonus against mind-affecting effects are sacred bonuses instead of morale bonuses; its fear immunity is unchanged.
- All Clerics gain Faithful Determination at level 10, granting a morale bonus on Will saves equal to the current Charisma modifier, with a minimum of +0.
- Clerics gain Divine Conduit at level 20. Their single-target and mass Cure and Inflict spells automatically maximize every die, they become immune to fear effects, and they gain +4 untyped Charisma. The maximization is limited to actual spell casts and does not affect potions, scrolls, channels, Heal, or Harm.
- Crusader Clerics replace Divine Conduit with Sacred War at level 20 and retain their existing bonus feat. Sacred War grants a sacred bonus to martial attack and damage rolls equal to one-third of the Crusader's Charisma modifier, rounded down, for good deities or a profane bonus for evil deities; neutral-deity Crusaders choose either version.
- Demonbane Priest Clerics gain Teamwork Bonus Feat selections at levels 4, 8, 12, and 16 instead of only levels 4 and 8, for four total bonus feats.
- Druids gain Beasts of Legends at level 20. It grants the Druid's animal companion +4 sacred Strength, Dexterity, and Constitution; grants the same bonuses to the Druid while shapeshifted; and grants +2 sacred Strength, Dexterity, and Constitution to every creature summoned by the Druid. Winter Child replaces Beasts of Legends with its own archetype capstone and does not receive both.
- Defender of the True World's Enemy of the Fey, Fey Stalker, and Feybane
  bonuses apply to evil outsiders as well as fey.
- Hunters choose Precise Shot or Outflank through Precise Companion at level 2, then receive the selection again at level 5 to gain the other feat. Both choices continue to ignore their normal prerequisites.
- Hunters gain Nurtured Growth at level 13, granting their animal companion +2 natural armor and +2 untyped Strength, Dexterity, and Constitution. The feature follows replacement animal companions through the standard pet system.
- Master Hunter retains its unlimited Animal Focus uses and additionally grants +2 dodge AC and +2 Base Attack Bonus to the Hunter and animal companion independently while that creature has an Animal Focus effect.
- Colluding Scoundrels gain +1d6 Sneak Attack at Hunter levels 5, 10, and 15, reaching +3d6. These use the standard Sneak Attack feature and stack normally with ranks from other sources.
- Divine Hounds replace the already-removed Master Hunter capstone with Eternal Judgment at level 20. Judgment can be used an unlimited number of times per day, and the Divine Hound and animal companion independently gain +2 dodge AC and +2 Base Attack Bonus while that creature has at least one Judgment active.
- Foresters gain Uncanny Dodge at level 2, receive Bonus Combat Feats at Hunter levels 2/6/10/14/18, and do not gain Nurtured Growth at level 13 because the archetype has no animal companion.
- Tandem Executioners gain their first Technique at level 1, for seven total selections at Hunter levels 1/4/7/10/13/16/19. At level 10 they gain Tandem Execution: once the Hunter and animal companion have both made a weapon attack against the same enemy, each deals +1d6 Sneak Attack precision damage with weapon attacks against that shared target. Attacking a different enemy or ending combat clears the coordination, and each Hunter-companion pair is tracked independently.
- Inquisitors gain Experienced Judgement at level 10, providing 2 additional Judgement uses per day and a +4 insight bonus to Wisdom. Faith Hunter, Judge, and Tactical Leader retain it because they retain a form of Judgement; Living Grimoire, Monster Tactician, Sacred Huntsmaster, and Sanctified Slayer do not gain it because they give up Judgement. At level 20, True Judgment also increases the simultaneous Judgement limit from three effects to four while retaining its existing death-judgement ability.
- All Magus characters gain Canny Defense at level 1. While wearing light or no armor and using no shield, they add their Intelligence modifier to AC as a dodge bonus capped by Magus class level; non-Sword Saints have no weapon requirement, while Sword Saint retains its original chosen-weapon requirement. Magus Bonus Feats are gained at levels 2/6/10/14/18 by the base class and every archetype.
- Uncanny Dodge is available as a visible Monk Bonus Feat from level 6 onward. It is added to the standard Monk pools and the tailored Scaled Fist, Zen Archer, and Student of Stone pools, while level-1 selections remain unchanged. The selectable entry grants Owlcat's hidden multiclass-safe tracker, so a character who already has Uncanny Dodge advances to Improved Uncanny Dodge normally.
- Ki Power: Perfect Self retains its DR 10/chaotic at Monk level 20 and adds 1d10 force damage to every unarmed hit. The Drunken Master version receives the same enhancement; manufactured weapons and natural weapons do not trigger the additional damage.
- Quarterstaff Masters gain Weapon Training (Quarterstaff) at Monk levels 7/10/13/16. It grants +1 attack and damage per rank with quarterstaves only, reaching +4, and uses the native Weapon Training bonus descriptor and marker without appearing in Fighter Weapon Training selections.
- Sensei Monks retain the standard Monk Evasion feature at level 2 and Fast Movement at level 3 while preserving every other Sensei feature replacement.
- Student of Stone's Strength of Stone gains improvements at Monk levels 9 and 15. Level 9 adds 1d6 bludgeoning damage to every unarmed hit; level 15 grants +4 inherent Strength and Constitution. The level-3 feature and its original bonuses remain unchanged.
- Traditional Monks gain Mastering the fundamentals at levels 7, 11, and 15. The three features respectively treat unarmed damage dice as one size category larger, grant +2 dodge AC, and add one attack at the highest attack bonus while Flurry of Blows is available under its normal armor, shield, and weapon restrictions.
- Zen Archers regain Evasion at level 2 and Improved Evasion at level 9. Rapid Shot, Manyshot, and Deflect Arrows are removed from all Zen Archer Monk Bonus Feat pools but remain ordinary feat choices; Deflect Arrows is instead granted automatically at level 7, and Improved Initiative is available in every bonus-feat pool. At level 15, Greater Ki Arrows treats unarmed strike damage dice as one size category larger and applies the same increase to longbows, composite longbows, shortbows, and composite shortbows. At level 20, One with the Arrow replaces Ki Power: Perfect Self, adding one attack at the highest attack bonus during bow Flurry of Blows and increasing bow critical threat range by 1.
- Every Paladin gains the actual Weapon Focus feat at level 1 with the favored weapon of the selected deity. The grant is automatic, appears for all Paladin archetypes, and satisfies normal Weapon Focus prerequisites.
- At level 3, Paladins who normally receive Mercy choose between keeping the full Mercy progression at levels 3/6/9/12/15/18 or replacing it with Fighter-compatible bonus combat feats at levels 3/9/15. Archetypes that already replace Mercy preserve that tradeoff, and Tortured Crusader retains only its four native Self-Sufficient selections at levels 2/8/12/16.
- Every Paladin's Lay on Hands pool is equal to Paladin class level plus Charisma modifier instead of half Paladin level plus Charisma modifier. This includes both Tortured Crusader resource variants, which now use Charisma for the pool; archetype features that grant extra uses continue to add on top.
- The Paladin spell list gains Aid as a 2nd-level spell and Freedom of Movement as a 4th-level spell.
- Martyr Paladins retain Divine Grace at level 2 while keeping all other Martyr feature additions and replacements unchanged.
- Stonelord Paladins retain Divine Grace at level 2 while keeping all other Stonelord feature additions and replacements unchanged.
- Warrior of the Holy Light activates every tier of Power of Faith and Shining Light as swift actions. Power of Faith's AC, attack, damage, and fear-save bonuses are sacred bonuses for both the Paladin and affected allies instead of morale bonuses.
- Rangers gain Combat Style feat selections at levels 2/4/6/8/10/12/14/16/18 instead of only 2/6/10/14/18. Ranger archetypes inherit the additional selections; Nomad and Stormwalker receive their fixed Archery-style versions at the new levels rather than unrestricted style choices.
- Espionage Experts gain +1d6 Sneak Attack at Ranger levels 7 and 14, reaching +2d6. At level 12 they gain Calculated Assault, providing a morale bonus on attack rolls equal to half their Charisma modifier, rounded down to a minimum of +0; at level 20 this improves to the full Charisma modifier.
- Flamewardens retain the base Ranger's Evasion at level 9 and Improved Evasion at level 16 while preserving every other Flamewarden addition and replacement.
- Nomads gain Exceptional Breed at levels 7, 12, and 17. Their horse gains +2 natural armor and +4 Constitution at level 7; +10 feet of untyped movement speed and +4 Dexterity at level 12; and +4 Strength plus one additional natural attack at its highest attack bonus during a full attack at level 17. The milestones are cumulative.
- Stormwalkers activate Wind Treader as a swift action instead of a standard action and gain Storm Resistance at level 7 for electricity resistance 10. They also retain the base Ranger's Quarry at level 11, Improved Evasion at level 16, and Improved Quarry at level 19 while preserving all other Stormwalker additions and replacements.
